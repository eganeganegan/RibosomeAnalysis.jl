Scientific methodology

Distances and contacts

For ligand atoms L, cutoff r, and residue i, the binding site is

B(L,r) = { i : min(distance(atom in i, atom in L)) <= r }.

The minimum uses heavy atoms by default. The software supports 3, 4, 5, and 8 Å as useful reporting thresholds, but none is a universal chemical definition. Contact persistence is the fraction of sampled frames satisfying the chosen cutoff. It is not a probability of binding.

Interaction geometry

Atomic contacts need coordinates only. Hydrogen bonds require explicit hydrogen coordinates and an explicit covalent bond list, then apply donor–acceptor distance and D–H–A angle thresholds. Protonation, tautomer state, water competition, and interaction energy remain unresolved. Ionic and aromatic analysis accepts explicit chemical groups rather than guessing them from atom names. Metal-mediated results are candidate geometries, not coordination bonds.

Alignment and RMSD

Needleman–Wunsch sequence alignment establishes candidate residue correspondence within user-confirmed chain pairs. Matching backbone atom names are fitted with a proper-rotation Kabsch solution. The reported RMSD is

sqrt(sum(||R x_i + t - y_i||^2) / N).

Coverage and unmatched residues accompany each fit. Equal residue numbers are never the sole basis for correspondence. Cross-species homology needs external biological validation.

Geometry

Centroid, radius of gyration, axis-aligned extent, pairwise-distance summaries, and local contact density describe selected coordinates. These are not an experimentally validated pocket volume, solvent accessibility, affinity, or free energy.

Dynamics and statistics

RMSD frames are aligned before measurement. RMSF uses the aligned ensemble mean. PCA is singular-value decomposition of mean-centered Cartesian features; components are mathematical variance directions and receive no automatic physical interpretation.

Periodic distances convert a Cartesian displacement to lattice coordinates and search the 27 translations surrounding the nearest lattice-coordinate image. This matches minimum-image distances for orthorhombic and reduced triclinic MD cells. Periodic contact persistence applies that distance independently to each atom pair and frame. Periodic handling is never enabled implicitly. Molecular unwrapping is topology dependent and remains the caller's responsibility before alignment-based observables.

Ordinary bootstrap intervals assume independent observations. Moving-block bootstrap intervals retain within-block time correlation. Effective sample size uses the positive initial autocorrelation sequence. Replica comparisons first reduce each independent replica to a summary, preventing frames from being misrepresented as independent simulations.

Physical units

All stored structure and trajectory distances are Angstrom. MDAnalysis reports positions in Angstrom under its standard conversion behavior. Array input declared as nanometers is multiplied by 10. Times are picoseconds. Every figure labels units explicitly. Stored unit-cell vectors receive the same length-unit conversion as coordinates.
