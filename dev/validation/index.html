Validation status

Passing unit tests are evidence about implementation behavior, not a claim of complete scientific validation.

Unit-tested functionality

  • BioStructures parsing of a controlled PDB fixture; preservation of atom, residue, chain, model, occupancy, B-factor, element, and hetero fields.
  • Exact synthetic KD-tree contact inclusion and monotonic cutoff behavior.
  • Kabsch recovery of a known rotation/translation and near-zero fitted RMSD.
  • Centroid, radius of gyration, extent, contact density, and pairwise counts for analytic coordinate sets.
  • Explicit-hydrogen 180° D–H–A geometry.
  • Nanometer-to-Angstrom conversion (factor 10).
  • RMSD invariance to rigid rotation/translation, RMSF non-negativity, contact persistence, distance series, PCA shapes, and deterministic clustering.
  • Autocorrelation normalization, effective sample-size bounds, bootstrap and moving-block-bootstrap behavior, and replica-level summaries.
  • Immutable result-directory behavior and machine-readable exports.

Cross-validated functionality

Structure parsing is delegated to BioStructures.jl 4.9.0, whose current API is used directly. Atom counts are checked independently through the BioStructures API in the public-data validation suite.

MDAnalysis 2.10.0 independently reproduced RMSD, aligned RMSF, and distance values for a deterministic analytic trajectory. The maximum observed difference from the Julia implementation was below 5e-8 Å. The checked-in reference also uses MDAnalysis to analyze the official PDB-format RCSB 1J7T entry. At a 5 Å cutoff, MDAnalysis and this package both report:

  • 356 paromomycin–environment atom-pair contacts;
  • 88 contacting non-ligand atoms;
  • 18 contacting residues; and
  • a minimum distance of approximately 2.51381 Å.

An end-to-end run on the compressed mmCIF RCSB 4V7U entry loaded 284,525 atoms in 104 author chains, selected ERY at author chain BA/residue 3136, and produced the standard metadata, summary, contact table, and visualization selections. At 5 Å it reported 283 atom-pair contacts, 96 contacting atoms, and 12 contacting residues. The input SHA-256 checksums and exact acquisition URLs are recorded in validation/public_structures.toml; the data are downloaded separately rather than bundled.

Functionality requiring further validation

  • A curated, versioned chemical-component database broader than the initial candidate mapping.
  • Hydrogen-bond assignments against a protonation/topology-aware reference.
  • Ionic/aromatic/metal candidates against quantum-chemical or curated interaction annotations.
  • MDAnalysis XTC/TRR/DCD streaming against GROMACS tools across unit-cell and periodic-boundary conventions. The in-memory trajectory calculations are cross-validated, but production-format periodic-boundary behavior is not.
  • Sequence correspondence across divergent species and paralogous chains.
  • Solvent-accessibility and pocket-occupancy algorithms (not implemented).
  • Time-lagged dimensionality reduction (not implemented).
  • Interactive molecular rendering (exports are provided instead).
  • Independent reproduction of publication figures from a real public ribosome dataset. Real-structure tables and selections are covered by the public-data suite; figure comparison remains qualitative.

Experimental or exploratory methods

Cartesian PCA, deterministic Lloyd clustering, aromatic ring orientation, and metal-mediated geometry are exploratory. They require domain-specific feature selection, convergence checks, sensitivity analysis, and external validation.

Interpretation boundaries

A geometric contact indicates proximity. A hydrogen-bond geometry indicates compatibility with user-supplied topology. Interaction persistence indicates cutoff occupancy over sampled frames. Structural stability describes limited coordinate observables. None alone establishes affinity, binding free energy, causality, clinical efficacy, or resistance mechanism.
